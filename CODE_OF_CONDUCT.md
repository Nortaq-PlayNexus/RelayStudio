# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:
- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving and accepting useful feedback
- Focusing on what is best for users and the project
- Showing empathy

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting comments, publishing others' private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
